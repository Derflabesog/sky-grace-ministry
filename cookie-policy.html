Cookie Policy

Last updated: 27 July 2026

This policy explains how the Sky Grace Jesus Ministry website may use cookies and other browser-storage technologies.

1. What Cookies Are

Cookies are small pieces of information that a website or third-party service may store in your browser. Websites may also use local storage or session storage to remember limited information on your device.

2. Essential Website Storage

Essential storage may be used to support secure administrator authentication, maintain a signed-in session, remember short-lived page state, and provide basic website functions. Disabling essential storage may prevent the administrator portal or some forms from working correctly.

3. Supabase Authentication

The administrator area uses Supabase for authentication and database services. Supabase may use browser storage to maintain a secure authenticated session for authorised administrators.

4. Embedded YouTube Content

Pages containing YouTube video players load content from YouTube. YouTube or Google may set cookies or receive technical information such as your IP address, browser details, and interactions with the video player. Their use of information is governed by their own policies.

5. Other Third-Party Links

The website may link to Facebook, WhatsApp, YouTube, and other external services. A link by itself does not place a cookie from that service, but the external service may use cookies after you open it.

6. Analytics and Advertising

This website does not currently claim to operate its own advertising-cookie or visitor-tracking programme. Embedded third-party services may still collect usage information according to their own settings and policies.

7. Managing Browser Storage

You can review, block, or delete cookies and site data through your browser settings. Blocking all cookies may affect video players, administrator login, or other website functions.

8. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated if the website begins using different services or storage technologies. The date at the top identifies the current version.

9. Contact Us

For questions about cookies or privacy, use the contact page or contact the Sky Grace Jesus Ministry church office at +264 81 232 0825.
